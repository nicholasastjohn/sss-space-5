Research standard

Three non-negotiables.

The list is useful only if a reader can trace what changed, inspect the evidence, and see where judgment or uncertainty entered the score.

01 · Public

Primary evidence first

Filings, official financial materials, government records, and mission documentation outrank narrative coverage.

02 · Dated

Every week is preserved

New evidence creates a new ranking. Historical records are never silently rewritten.

03 · Honest

Missing means missing

Material source gaps make a company ineligible; they are not filled with confident-sounding guesses.

Important boundary

Research, not a trading signal.

SSS Space 5 does not model valuation or expected return and makes no recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. It uses lawful public sources only—never employer, customer, classified, controlled, proprietary, procurement-sensitive, export-controlled, or material nonpublic information.
